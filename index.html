How to play ?

  • Arrow Keys: Move safely on the edges.
  • Goal: Trap the enemy in 20% of the area or less to win the game!
  • Space + Arrow Keys: Go into the empty space to start drawing.
  • Close Area: Connect your line back to an edge or your own path to capture the area!
  • Watch Out: Don't hit enemies or bullets while drawing, or you will lose a life!
